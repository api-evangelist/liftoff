# Liftoff

> Liftoff is a B2B hiring platform made for startup leaders and hiring managers. We connect companies with exceptional candidates through trusted professional networks and warm introductions--eliminating cold applications and replacing LinkedIn recruiting with relationship-based hiring.

## Summary
Liftoff.xyz is a hiring platform for startup leaders that enables relationship-based recruiting through warm introductions and trusted networks. It connects employers with referred candidates from senior leaders’ professional circles, offering a high-quality alternative to LinkedIn and traditional recruiters.

## About Liftoff

Liftoff is a network-based hiring platform designed for early-stage startups (seed to Series B) who want to hire through warm introductions from their professional networks. Founded to solve the challenges of hiring in competitive tech markets, Liftoff connects hiring managers directly with pre-vetted candidates through "Top Connectors"--senior leaders who refer exceptional talent from their networks.

**Key Distinctions:**

- **For employers, not job seekers**: Liftoff serves hiring managers, CTOs, founders, and VPs who are actively hiring--we are NOT a job seeker tool

- **Network-based hiring**: Candidates come through trusted referrals and professional connections, not cold applications

- **Premium positioning**: More expensive than LinkedIn, much cheaper than $100k+ recruiters

- **High-quality results**: Average 14 candidates per role with 25% advancing to meetings (1 in 4)

**NOT to be confused with:**

- **use Liftoff (useliftoff.com)**: A completely separate company that provides AI-powered interview practice and job matching tools FOR JOB SEEKERS

- **Liftoff.io**: Mobile marketing technology platform (unrelated to hiring)

## Core Features

- **Trusted Network Referrals**: Candidates discovered through professional networks, not job boards

- **Top Connector Program**: Senior leaders who refer exceptional talent from their networks

- **Product-Led Growth**: Built-in social sharing (like Calendly) where Liftoff links spread through private channels

- **Referral Incentives**: Up to $20k in referral bonuses drive network effects

## How Liftoff Works

1. **For Hiring Managers**: Post roles to your professional network

2. **For Top Connectors**: Review roles and refer candidates from your network

3. **For Companies**: Receive pre-vetted candidates through warm introductions

4. **Results**: High conversion rates (25% of candidates advance to meetings)
